WHY THE LOGGER IS DARK
Follows system, and free-weight areas are dim — but the deeper reason is that a phone held between sets at arm's length in a mirror-lined room is a glare problem. The logger was validated in both themes per the Foundations decision; this is the one that gets used.
THE ACTIVE SET IS THE ONLY TAP TARGET
Completed sets are records, not controls — they read at 16px and are editable only by long-press. The current set gets 20px numerals, a 44px Log button and the accent wash. One thumb, no aiming. Weight and reps step by ±2.5kg and ±1 on swipe rather than opening a keyboard.
OFFLINE IS THE DEFAULT ASSUMPTION
Gyms have basements and thick walls. Every set, note and voice memo writes locally first and syncs opportunistically — there is no “saving” state and no error if the network is gone, because a coach cannot stop a session to deal with connectivity. The only visible sync signal is a quiet line at the bottom, and it says what is queued rather than what failed.
SESSION COMPLETION IS ONE TAP
End → the session is verified, the credit decrements, the member's app updates, and the note is attached. No separate verification screen, which is precisely why 31 sessions accumulated unverified in the old flow: verification was a second visit to a different place.

THE 30–60 SECOND CLAIM, ACCOUNTED FOR
Open Ahmad → Nutrition → Generate: 3 taps, 4 seconds of compute. Reviewing seven days at a glance: ~20 seconds. One swap if he objects to kofta: 2 taps. Assign: 1 tap. Total around 35 seconds for a plan that respects his allergy, his dislike of fish, his training days and his current cut. The target is met by not asking, not by making the form faster.
LOCAL FOOD IS NOT A GARNISH
Mansaf, kofta, flatbread, dates. A nutrition product for Amman that proposes quinoa bowls gets ignored in week one — the food has to be what people actually eat, with the macros computed against local preparations rather than a US database.
THE CLINICAL GATE, UNCHANGED
This plan auto-assigns because Ahmad has no flagged condition. Had he one, the same screen would show “needs review by a qualified professional” in place of the Assign button, with the plan preserved and routed. Veyro defines no clinical criteria — the routing point is built, the rules arrive from whoever holds clinical accountability in each market, and the UX does not change when they do.
AI IS MARKED, EVIDENCED, AND STOPS SHORT
The indigo panel is color.semantic.ai — system-owned, so a white-label tenant cannot make generated content look like the club's own prescription. “See the numbers” opens the derivation. Nothing is assigned without the coach's tap.
