04 · DESIGN SYSTEM COMPLIANCE & BLUEPRINT TRACEABILITY

What the phase actually proved

ZERO ONE-OFF TOKENS · {{ totalCanvases }} CANVASES
Across five surfaces, four densities, both themes and two writing directions, no screen required a colour, space, radius, elevation or type value outside v1. The only system change in Phase 9 was color.semantic.ai, which you commissioned rather than a screen demanding.
FIVE MODULES, ZERO NEW PATTERNS
Scheduling, payments, booking, automations and onboarding all composed from the ten canonical patterns. The automation builder was the real test — the temptation was a node-graph canvas, and the sentence form reused AutomationRule instead. If any of these five had needed a new pattern, the foundation was wrong.
RTL PRODUCED REAL LAYOUT CHANGES
Cart 250→288, rail 184→212, set-logger expression merged into one isolated run, meal cards taller for spelled-out macros, POS tax note onto two lines. Every one came from measuring the Arabic string, and each is documented beside the canvas that needed it.
BLUEPRINT TRACEABILITY
Every canvas traces to an approved inventory entry; no screen was invented. Jordan-specific requirements from §75 appear where they belong — JoFotara in the Command Center queue, POS receipt and onboarding checklist; CliQ as a payment method in POS. No V2 or Enterprise functionality was exposed: multi-brand, franchise hierarchy and the marketplace are absent, not stubbed.
ONE NEW CONTENT REQUIREMENT FILED
Arabic member-facing copy is gendered. Every second-person sentence in the member app requires member gender as a rendering input, with a neutral fallback when unknown. Not a design defect — a content and data requirement the English build never surfaced, and one that affects the string catalogue rather than the components.
PERMISSIONS: DEMONSTRATED, NOT EXHAUSTIVE
Four of nine roles are drawn — Owner, Club Manager, Receptionist, Coach. Sales, Nutritionist, Accountant and Regional Manager are specified by the absent-not-greyed rule and the requestable-action pattern but not drawn. The rule is proven; those four compositions are not.
