ONE CARD OWNS THE VIEWPORT
“Lower body at 09:45” at 30px is the whole answer to what should I do now. Everything else — payment, food, classes — sits below it. A member who opens the app in a lift and closes it three seconds later got what they came for.
LOGGING IS ONE TAP, SWAPPING IS THREE
Ate it is a 44px button on the meal itself — no confirmation, no macro entry, undoable for a minute. Swap opens a sheet with three real alternatives already filtered against his allergy and his dislike of fish, each with its own Ate it: swap and log are the same tap. Search, photo and barcode are below the alternatives, not above them, because they are the fallback and not the path.
HONEST ABOUT TRADE-OFFS, NOT PRESCRIPTIVE
The lentil option is shown with “18g less protein” in warning rather than hidden or ranked last. Members eat what they want; the app's job is to make the consequence visible and log it accurately. Hiding worse options produces a food diary nobody fills in.
THE PAYMENT CARD IS NOT A DUNNING NOTICE
“Your card expired · you can still train · sort it whenever.” Warning severity, not error — because nothing is actually blocked and the tone should match the stake. The Admin side knows it is retry three of a dunning sequence with 14 days of grace remaining. Ahmad does not need to.
SHEETS, NOT SCREENS
Swap, booking, payment and food search are all sheets over the page — the context stays visible behind, dismissal is a swipe, and the back stack never grows. Two levels maximum. Nothing in the member app is a navigation destination that has to be navigated back out of.
