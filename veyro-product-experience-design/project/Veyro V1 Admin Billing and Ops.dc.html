STATES · ADM-PAY-03
Paid — status badge in success, JoFotara reference shown, Take payment replaced by Receipt. Refunded — original preserved, refund shown as a linked negative document, never an edit. Disputed — evidence deadline banner. Void — struck, reason and author recorded. JoFotara rejected — the field at fault named, with a fix-and-resubmit action.
IMMUTABILITY
Issued invoices are never edited. Corrections are credit notes with their own number and a link to the original. There is no edit affordance on this screen at any permission level — which is a design decision, not a missing feature, and the reason the timeline can be trusted as an audit trail.
PERMISSIONS
Accountant and Club Manager see everything here. Reception sees the invoice and Take payment but not the ⋯ menu (void, credit note, resend to JoFotara). Coach has no route to this screen at all.

Buyer tax number is required for business sales. When a member is registered as a company, the field becomes mandatory at the point of sale rather than failing later at submission — which is what caused this month's three rejections, and is now prevented upstream.
Global shape, local content. This screen is TaxIntegration — JoFotara is the Jordan adapter. A UK tenant sees HMRC MTD in the same frame with different fields. Nothing here is hard-coded to Jordan except the adapter's own configuration.
