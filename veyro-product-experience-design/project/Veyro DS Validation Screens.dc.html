DESIGN SYSTEM v1 · STEP 6 — VALIDATION SCREENS · §71–§74

Proof the system composes

Six screens assembled only from Steps 2–5 — no new colours, no new spacing, no one-off components. Each names the parts it used. Two are rendered in Arabic RTL, and the Arabic ones are sized from the Arabic string rather than the English one. Clears DS-P0-8.

WHAT WOULD COUNT AS A FAILURE HERE
Needing a value outside the token scale, a component outside the library, or a rule bent to make a screen work. Any of those means the system is under-specified, not that the screen is special. None occurred — but two gaps did, and they are recorded at the end rather than quietly patched.

FIVE RULES VISIBLE AT ONCE
1 The banner answers all four error questions — what, what is affected, is it safe, what now.
2 Card is disabled with its condition stated, the legitimate use of disabled from §57.
3 The sale still completes. An outage in one method never blocks trading.
4 Tax stays a visible line even at JOD 4.06.
5 Nothing here is new — banner, cart, MoneyValue, Button states, all from the library.
TWO GAPS THIS SCREEN EXPOSED
DS-P2-1 · a queued-order tray. The banner says card orders are queued, but no component shows the queue or lets staff reconcile it when the provider returns. Composable from DataGrid plus TaskRow — but it is not specified, so it is logged rather than assumed.

DS-P2-2 · degraded-method precedence. When one method is down, which becomes the default primary? Cash here, but the rule is not written. A tenant with no cash drawer needs a different answer.
Both are P2 — real gaps, neither blocking hi-fi. Recording them is the point of building validation screens instead of swatch sheets.

RESULT

What the six screens established

PASSED
Zero new colours, spacing values, radii or type sizes. Zero new components. All four densities exercised, both directions rendered, and the two hardest cases — an Arabic touch surface and a degraded-service checkout — composed without special-casing.
GAPS FOUND · BOTH P2
DS-P2-1 queued-order tray · DS-P2-2 degraded-method precedence. Both surfaced only because screen 6 was chosen to be a failure case. A happy-path POS screen would have passed and taught nothing.
STILL UNPROVEN
Screen-reader behaviour, real Dynamic Type at XXL, an actual 100k-member grid, and a genuine offline-to-online sync. These are build-time validations. Naming them keeps the verdict honest — VALIDATE-1 stands.
