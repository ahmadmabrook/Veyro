PHASE 8 · ARABIC RTL · COMMAND CENTER

Command Center — العربية

Not the English canvas flipped. Written in Arabic first, then measured — the heading, the rail, the metric labels and the exception copy are all longer, and the layout was dimensioned against them. Below the screen: the eight places where mirroring alone would have produced a defect.

THE MEASUREMENT THAT DROVE THIS LAYOUT
The Arabic rail needs 212px against the English 184px — that is +28 on the Compact-density figure, not on the 240 the shell uses at Default. And the metric row needs 20% more height because “الإيرادات المحصّلة · من بداية الشهر” cannot sit on one line at this width. Neither was a surprise — Foundations §05 said containers get sized from the Arabic string. This is the first screen where that cost real pixels.

COPY WRITTEN, NOT TRANSLATED
“يحتاج انتباهك الآن” rather than a literal rendering of “Needs you now”. “استمرارية العضوية” for the retention category, because a direct translation of “retention” reads like HR jargon to an Arabic-speaking gym manager. JoFotara appears as جوفوترة — the transliteration Jordanian accountants actually use — while Veyro and ⌘K stay Latin because that is how they are spoken.
NUMERALS · TENANT SETTING, APPLIED WHOLE
Nadi Group runs Arabic-Indic, so counts, dates, times and percentages all use ٠١٢٣. Money and Latin identifiers keep IBM Plex Mono, LTR-isolated — JOD 41,280.00, INV-20418, ···4417 — because that is the form on their invoices, statements and JoFotara submissions, and a member disputing a charge must see the same string in both places. Every other numeral follows the tenant setting in Plex Sans Arabic with tabular figures, since Plex Mono has no Arabic. One rule for both files, not a per-component judgement.
WHAT THE TYPE RULES COST
Line-height 1.75–1.85 against the Latin 1.6, weights up one step (450→500, 500→600), zero tracking anywhere, and no size below 13px — which is why the metric eyebrows here are 11.5px Arabic sans rather than the 9.5px mono the English canvas uses. Same information, more room, deliberately.
