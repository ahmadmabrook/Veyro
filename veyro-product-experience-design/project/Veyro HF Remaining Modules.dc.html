Typing the amount is the only place in Veyro that asks for a keystroke to confirm. Money leaving the business is the one action where a mis-click cannot be undone by an undo toast.
The four confirmation tiers, as used here
1 · Nothing. Sending a payment link, adding a note, checking someone in.
2 · Undo toast, 30s. Resolving an exception, logging a set, marking a meal eaten.
3 · Typed confirmation. Refunds, cash adjustments, deleting a member record.
4 · Second person. Refunds over a threshold, permission changes, biometric removal, sensitive export.
Tier 3 and 4 compose — this refund is both.

Sentences, not a node graph. A club manager configures dunning here; nobody draws a flowchart. The NEVER block is not a rule the user wrote — it is the boundary the system imposes, shown so they can trust the rest.
