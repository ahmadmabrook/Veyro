LIKELYThat most of the 31 are genuine. Omar's historical verification rate is 94%, so the 1,240 figure is an upper bound, not an expectation.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

"This is wrong" records the disagreement against the insight with a reason, and the same reading is not raised again for 30 days. An interpretation a manager cannot contradict is not an interpretation.
WHY THREE CONFIDENCE BANDS AND NOT A PERCENTAGE
A number like "82% confident" reads as measured precision about something that is a judgement. Three named bands — counted, likely, cannot separate — say the same thing without borrowing authority the reasoning does not have.
STATES · ADM-AI-01
Stale inputs withholds the insight entirely rather than reasoning from old figures. Insufficient data says which comparison could not be made. Disputed shows the manager's objection above the claim. Acted on shows what was done, by whom, and what changed afterwards.
PERMISSIONS
Owner and Regional see cross-club derivations. A Club Manager sees their own club's arithmetic. Reception never reaches this screen — an insight nobody can act on is noise. The indigo is color.semantic.ai and never inherits a tenant brand.
