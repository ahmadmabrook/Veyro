RESPONSIVE · LOADING · INTERACTION

Behaviour engineering needs

bp.xl · 1600+
Content caps at 1440 and centres. The AI panel and the two chart cards sit in a right column at 480 rather than the attention queue stretching — a 1,600px-wide exception row is harder to read, not easier.
bp.lg · 1200–1599 · TARGET
As drawn. Rail expanded at 184, metrics in four columns, AI panel and charts share a row below the queue. This is the layout to build first.
bp.md · 900–1199
Rail collapses to 56px icons with labels on hover and focus. Metrics go 2×2. AI panel and charts stack full-width. The attention queue never collapses — it is the reason the page exists.
bp.sm AND BELOW
Rail becomes a sheet from a menu button. Metrics scroll horizontally as a single row of cards — the one place horizontal scroll is right, because they are peers with no comparison axis. Attention rows stack their actions and the primary goes full-width. Charts drop to a sparkline plus the stated conclusion in words, since a 12-bar comparison at 360px is decoration.
LOADING · FOUR INDEPENDENT REQUESTS
Shell and rail render immediately from cached role and scope. Then, in parallel and each with its own skeleton at true final height: metrics, attention queue, charts, AI panel. No spinners anywhere. The queue arrives before the charts by priority. If the AI panel is slowest it shows a skeleton, never a delay on anything else.
INTERACTION · KEY BEHAVIOURS
Queue is one tab stop; ↑↓ moves rows, ⏎ runs the primary action, S snoozes. Resolving collapses the row in place with a 30-second undo toast; the open count decrements visibly. New items arrive as a “3 new” pill at the top and never inject into a list being read. Scope change re-fetches everything and states the new scope in the heading, not only the chrome.
DESIGN SYSTEM CHANGE LOG · §51

One extension, filed not smuggled

DS-CL-01 · MISSING VARIANT · MetricCell.stale
ISSUE
Canvas 5 needs individual metrics to show their own freshness. The system specified partial data at page level (a banner naming what is stale) but gave MetricCell no way to carry provenance, so a stale figure and a live one were visually identical below the banner.
WHY THE SYSTEM COULD NOT EXPRESS IT
A page banner is the right container for the explanation but the wrong granularity for the data. Mixing fresh and stale numbers in one grid with a single caveat above them invites exactly the misreading the money rules exist to prevent — a manager quoting a stale collections figure as current.
CLASSIFICATION & SOLUTION
Missing variant, not a defect. Smallest reusable extension: MetricCell gains freshness: live | stale | unavailable. Stale demotes the value from text.primary to text.secondary and replaces the comparison line with a timestamp in warning. No new token, no new colour — existing semantic tokens in a new arrangement. Generalises to any panel with mixed provenance.
SCREENS AFFECTED
Command Center canvas 5; any future report or dashboard with mixed-freshness panels.
ACCESSIBILITY IMPACT
Positive and required: freshness must be in the accessible name — “collected month to date, 41,280 dinars, as of 08:00” — because the visual demotion alone is a colour-and-weight signal.
RTL IMPACT
None structural. The timestamp is bidi-isolated like every other time value.
Nothing else required an extension. Every other element on all five canvases resolved to an existing component, token and state — which is the actual result being reported here, more than the screens themselves.
