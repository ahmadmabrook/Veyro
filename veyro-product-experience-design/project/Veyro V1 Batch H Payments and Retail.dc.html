ADM-PAY-06 · DUNNING · OWNER & ACCOUNTANT
When a payment fails
Retry the card twice, one day apart, at 06:00.
Draft a message after the final failure and hold it for approval.
Allow access for 14 days past the due date.
Raise a Command Center exception when 3 or more fail in one run.
NEVER, WHATEVER THE RULES SAY
Never block access without a person deciding · never message outside 09:00–20:00 · never send more than one reminder a week · never charge a card that has been removed.
Sentence form, same as the automation builder. The NEVER block is not configurable per-tenant — it is the floor beneath every dunning policy.

ADM-POS-03 · DISCOUNTS · OWNER ONLY
Staff discount · 20% on retail. Any staff member may apply it to their own purchase.
Goodwill · up to 10% on anything. Reception may apply without approval.
Anything over 10% needs a manager present at the till. Not a code — a person.
Open day · 15% off memberships, valid 13–20 August, expires automatically.
The 10% threshold here is what makes POS-06 ask for a manager. One number, configured once, enforced at every till.
Discount codes were considered and rejected for V1: a code that circulates among staff is indistinguishable from no control at all. Approval is a person, and it is recorded.
