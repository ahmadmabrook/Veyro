WHY THIS FITS ON A PHONE
A twelve-week programme is 36 sessions and roughly 180 exercise entries. It fits because the coach never enters 180 things: a template supplies the structure, week blocks share a day pattern, and progression is a rule rather than 12 hand-typed weights. The screen edits a pattern, not a spreadsheet.
THE LIMITATION IS ENFORCED, NOT DISPLAYED
Adding an exercise that conflicts with a recorded limitation flags at the point of adding, with the reason and the intake date. It does not block — a coach may have a reason — but it cannot be silent. Veyro defines no clinical rule here; it surfaces what a qualified person recorded.
STATES · CCH-08
Blank offers templates first and an empty programme second. Unsaved autosaves every change with a quiet timestamp; there is no Save-or-lose modal. Assigned locks structural edits and offers "adjust from here" so a mid-programme change does not rewrite completed weeks. Conflict when two coaches edit the same shared template.
