STATES · ADM-CC-06
Unassigned as drawn. Assigned to me shows the owner and adds Release. Assigned to someone else keeps every action visible but read-only, with "Nadia is handling this" and a Take over that records the handover. Resolved shows the outcome, resolver and elapsed time, and offers Reopen. Snoozed shows the reason and return date.
PERMISSIONS
A Club Manager sees only the two members at their club and a JOD total scoped to them. Reception can open the sheet but has no batch action — only Take payment per member. An exception a role cannot act on never enters their queue in the first place.
KEYBOARD
Opens with focus on the heading, not the primary action — a destructive default under a returning Enter key is how batch messages get sent by accident. Esc closes and returns focus to the originating row.
