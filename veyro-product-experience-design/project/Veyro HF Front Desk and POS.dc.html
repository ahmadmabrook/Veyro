PHASE 9 · SYSTEMATIC PRODUCTION · SURFACE 3 & 4 OF 5

Front Desk & Point of Sale

Touch density, light theme only, one modal depth. These are the two surfaces where a second of hesitation is visible to a queue of people — so the design question is not what fits on screen but what the hands do without looking.

THE RULE THAT SHAPES BOTH SCREENS
Focus returns to the scan field after every action, always. A receptionist never clicks into a search box twice in a row. Every completion — check-in resolved, sale finished, exception dismissed — puts the cursor back where the next member's card will land. That single behaviour is worth more than any layout decision here.

Where confirmation exists, and why
None on a routine sale — the receipt is the confirmation and Void is available for 90 seconds.
None on a membership sale, but the terms and first billing date appear in the cart before payment.
Manager approval on a discount over 10% or any refund — a different person, not a louder dialog.
Typed amount on a cash refund, because the number is the thing being risked.
