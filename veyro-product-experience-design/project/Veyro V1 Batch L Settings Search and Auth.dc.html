ACTIONS COME SECOND, DELIBERATELY
The member is found first, then what you can do to them. Ranking actions above entities means a mistyped name can trigger a payment on the wrong person — the entity is the safety anchor and the actions inherit it.
PERMISSION-FILTERED AT QUERY TIME
Reception typing the same query gets the member and Take payment, but no Freeze and no invoice row. Results are filtered in the query, not hidden in the response — a search index that returns records a role cannot see is a data leak with a UI in front of it.
EMPTY AND SCALE STATES
Empty shows four recent members and three suggested actions, not a blank field. At 100,000 members the shape is unchanged — the query is server-side and capped at five per section with "see all 412 matches" opening the filtered table.
