## What is delegated

Four things are decided here. The first three are delegated; the fourth records conditions as spent and delegates nothing. Each delegation runs to the orchestrating Claude Code session, which is the sole repository writer, and to no other agent.

(1) **Routine confirmation.** Where a published record reserves to the owner only the confirmation that an otherwise-authorized routine step may proceed, that confirmation is delegated and needs no separate owner prompt. The delegated confirmations are exactly: selecting and prioritizing the next eligible task; resolving a defect or failing test; deciding how to address a reviewer finding; managing a retry within a budget that an existing record already records as available; reconciling requirement and design documents against the four pinned baselines; running validation; and publishing an otherwise-authorized, independently reviewed change by non-force push to `main` with successful exact-SHA push CI. This delegates the confirmation only. It enlarges no budget, relaxes no gate, widens no scope, supplies no authority the underlying record lacks, and owner silence remains not approval. “Eligible” and “available” are read against the live records: a task, lane or budget that a record closes, exhausts, reserves or leaves ambiguous is neither, and an ambiguous count is raised, not resolved, here. The publication clause reaches a change only where that change's own authority already exists; it gives no authority over a controlled path (an accepted ADR that `Covers` it is still required), a frozen path, or any `OWNER-*` record.

(2) **Source interpretation.** Resolving an ordinary ambiguity between the four pinned baselines is delegated. It is a reading of pinned text, not an edit: it changes no pinned byte, it requires the relevant hash to be verified first (`tools/governance/source_baseline.py`; `CLAUDE.md`, “Canonical sources”), and it fails closed on mismatch. Where `MOD-004.md` Unresolved owner-reserved choices item 3 (line 152) says “Resolve from the Blueprint first, otherwise the owner decides”, the delegation reaches only the Blueprint-resolvable part; a value the Blueprint does not supply stays with the owner. No paragraph number, record name, hash or review ID may be inferred or invented to close a gap; an unsourced gap is raised, not filled. A conflict that the pinned texts themselves leave open, such as the Appendix B PLT-01-R06 versus WHT-01 conflict for ADM-SET-06 (`MOD-004.md` line 118; ADR-0002 D15.10 Q1-Q2, lines 321-322), is not an ordinary ambiguity (see “What is reserved and nondelegable”).

(3) **The governance branch that already exists.** Where a published record places a step with “a governance or `OWNER-*` decision” — as `MOD-004.md` Unresolved owner-reserved choices item 4 (line 153) and ADR-0002 Unresolved owner-reserved choices item 4 (line 353) both do, each adding “none exists” — the governance branch of that wording is available now: an ACCEPTED ADR under ADR-0001, independently reviewed, suffices for that branch and needs no additional owner record. This decides no such ADR's content and accepts none. It makes no module Ready and authorizes no code or capability activation, and it does not reach any matter under “What is reserved and nondelegable”: by this branch an ADR cannot change the strict module sequence, unlock MOD-005 or decide an owner-reserved choice. **OWNER-Q1 stays PENDING** with its recorded default standing — no interim access log in S1 (`MOD-004.md` line 157; ADR-0002 D6.4, line 129) — and the governance branch is not a route around that default. **OWNER-Q2** (the Appendix F ¶8741 lint timing, `MOD-004.md` line 146) likewise stays PENDING. This record answers neither.

(4) **Runner-era lanes recorded as spent.** The operational-admission, quarantine, re-arm, fixed-ID dispatch, conditional-reload, runner-hash-equality and runner-gate conditions enumerated in the “Decision and scope” paragraph of `OWNER-2026-10-29-minimal-local-runner-architecture.md` (line 12, clauses (1)-(6) and the sentence on the S0 `runner_gate` subject and runner-hash equality) were addressed to a runner that is now retired as an execution path. This record records them SPENT: not delegated, not revived, not reinterpreted. No step those conditions gated may be taken by relabelling it routine or delegated. They remain historical truth for old-runner events, as `OWNER-2026-10-29` itself says. Every product predicate in the same records keeps its full meaning and status, including all consumed one-write and one-review limits, every S0 subject other than the retired `runner_gate` subject and runner-hash equality, and all scenario rows; in that record's words, “all other S0 subjects, consumed one-write/one-review limits and scenario rows retain their meaning and status”.
