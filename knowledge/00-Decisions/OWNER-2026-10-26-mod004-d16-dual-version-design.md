The design must distinguish independently versioned **target operation heads** `(command, target_scope_kind, operation_version)` from **authority-policy heads** `(command, authority_scope_kind, authority_policy_version)`. Both heads and their bindings are database-owned authoritative state in the proposed DB-owned, function-only command design. An `operator_grant` is effective only against its authority head. A request/result is effective only against its target operation head. A reviewed, immutable, exact-version operation-authority binding connects the two, with an enforced scope/target/subject/effect envelope. Explicit C10/C11/E03 TENANT-to-PLATFORM governance/emergency bindings are a **permitted design option requiring later exact-candidate Opus approval**, not pre-approved executable authority; a PLATFORM grant never becomes a wildcard C02/C03 TENANT lifecycle permission. Equality of numbers in the two namespaces confers nothing. O6's prohibition on PLATFORM permission grants covering tenant lifecycle commands is unchanged.

The following conditions from the independent **advisory** Opus challenge are mandatory design criteria, not pre-approved implementation. They cover all ten findings: B1→1, B2→2, B3→3, B4/B7→4, B5/B6/B8→5, and B9/B10→6.

1. **Termination liveness.** Enabling C10 TENANT issuance requires viable C11 TENANT ordinary termination and E03 TENANT emergency termination unless a later explicit owner decision removes E03 from the required design. For every live, unended tenant grant, assess termination reachability after every authority/operation transition, including DISABLED transitions. Issuance may be stopped reduce-only; ordinary disablement of the last viable termination path while grants remain live is refused. If a separately authorized security containment action must disable a defective termination path, it must atomically stop issuance, preserve immutable evidence, record the stranded exposure and escalate surviving grants; it grants no replacement positive authority. This record does not itself authorize such containment.
2. **Per-subject quorum.** C11 viability must exclude each grant's grantee human and still meet the independent approval quorum; E03 needs an ACTIVE eligible emergency human distinct from that subject. Use relevant C11/E03 grant TTL and commissioning margins, not an aggregate C10 quorum count. Structural viability does not claim humans or external services are available.
3. **Genesis pinning.** Genesis grants are pinned to their closure-time authority-policy version/digest and restricted to C10/C12/C13 PLATFORM bindings explicitly marked genesis-eligible. No C10 TENANT reach, policy broadening, TTL extension or reopening genesis. Incompatible changes fail closed; X5 lost-quorum/commissioning-lapse recovery remains owner-reserved.
4. **Compatibility and stale action control.** Grant compatibility requires equal digest over every permission-relevant authority field, including class, target/subject envelope and enforced effect ceiling, with downward-only TTL changes and original expiry preserved. Compatibility carries forward only grants effective at the immediately superseded head; inert grants cannot resurrect. Compatibility never preserves pending requests or approvals: any operation head, authority head or binding change voids them and requires fresh AAL-3 action attestations.
5. **Explicit ownership and action binding.** Operation heads own required approvals, request/approval TTL, schema and a defined expected aggregate version. Authority heads own permission class, envelope, scope/effect ceiling and grant TTL. The effective-permission predicate must check a grant at its authority head, an exact binding to the current authority and operation heads, and matching digests. C10 TENANT action hashes bind its TENANT operation version, governing C10 PLATFORM authority version, the created C02/C03 TENANT grant's authority version and binding digest; C11/E03 bind the source grant and concrete tenant key. Approval, execution and denial behavior must fail closed on stale or ambiguous versions. This lineage may propose the aggregate and `expected_version` semantics for each command extending O13, but must mark them **OPEN** until separately owner-decided. Hashes must define explicit typed inputs and fail closed while any required value is unset; no implicit constant or claimed implementation before that decision.
6. **Atomic transitions and grant ending.** Specify the Z0 authority-head/binding entry with owner-record, immutable-audit evidence and audit-integration decision references; dependency closure and a total lock order with in-flight executions; one atomic multi-row head/binding/enablement transition. Refuse a transition that leaves C10 TENANT issuance enabled without viable C11/E03 termination, or disables the last viable termination path for live grants, except under separately authorized containment as in condition 1; stopping issuance itself remains allowed. C11, E03 and the labeled Z0 F25 entry may end an unexpired, unended grant even if its version is inert; TENANT C11/E03 must bind a C02/C03 TENANT source grant of the same tenant key. Ending is reduce-only, not authorization compatibility.
